How do I find my FAST NUCES section timetable? ▾
Select your department (CS, SE, AI, DS, or CY) and section (e.g., A, B, C) in the Section Timetable tab. The schedule immediately displays all class slots, timings, and room numbers.
Which campus and degrees are supported? ▾
Fastable is exclusively tailored for FAST-NUCES Islamabad Campus (H-11/4) and its computing degree programs: BS CS, BS SE, BS AI, BS DS, and BS CYS.
How does custom course clash detection work? ▾
Switch to the "Custom Timetable & Clash Detector" tab. Select any courses from the catalog and add them. Fastable immediately detects any overlapping time slots and flags conflicting classes.

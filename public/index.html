Frequently Asked Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Helpful answers about FAST Islamabad timetable access and custom schedule creation.

How do I find my FAST NUCES section timetable? ▾
In the Batch Timetable tab, select your Department (e.g. CS), your Batch year (e.g. 2024), and your Section (e.g. A), then click Show Timetable. The schedule immediately renders your weekly lectures, lab slots, and room allocations from the live timetable sheet.
Which campus and degree programs are supported? ▾
Fastable is built specifically for FAST-NUCES Islamabad Campus (H-11/4) and covers all computing faculties: BS Computer Science (CS), BS Software Engineering (SE), BS Artificial Intelligence (AI), BS Data Science (DS), and BS Cyber Security (CY).
How does the custom course selection work? ▾
Switch to the Custom Course Selection tab, filter by department or batch, and type to search for any course. Click it to add to your list. Once you click Show Timetable, Fastable displays all sessions for your selected courses. Use the Clash Detector button to check for overlaps.
Can I use Fastable offline on my phone? ▾
Yes, download the official Android APK release (v1.0.7) to save your schedules locally, view campus maps, and receive notifications before lectures begin.

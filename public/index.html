How do I find my FAST NUCES section timetable?
To find your section timetable:
  1. Select your degree program (CS, SE, AI, DS, or CYS).
  2. Choose your semester / batch (e.g. Semester 4).
  3. Pick your section code (e.g. BCS-4A).
  4. Or type your course code (e.g. CS2001) or teacher name directly into the search bar.
The grid will immediately filter to display your weekly classes, timings, and classroom numbers.

Frequently Asked Questions

Frequently Asked Questions About FAST-NUCES Timetables

Everything you need to know about timetable lookups, room numbers, clash detection, and using Fastable at FAST-NUCES.

How do I find my FAST NUCES section timetable?
To find your section timetable, simply use the interactive timetable tool at the top of Fastable:
  1. Choose your campus (Islamabad, Lahore, Karachi, Peshawar, or CFD).
  2. Select your academic department (such as CS, SE, AI, DS, CYS, EE, or BBA).
  3. Pick your batch or semester and select your specific section code (e.g., BCS-4A).
  4. Alternatively, type your section code, course name, or teacher name into the live search bar.
The grid will immediately filter down to show your weekly lecture slots, timings, and assigned room numbers.
Which campuses are supported on Fastable?
Fastable fully supports all five official campuses of FAST-NUCES (National University of Computer and Emerging Sciences) in Pakistan:
  • Islamabad Campus (ISB) – H-11/4 Campus
  • Lahore Campus (LHR) – Faisal Town Campus
  • Karachi Campus (KHI) – Malir Main Campus & City Campus
  • Peshawar Campus (PWR) – Hayatabad Campus
  • Chiniot-Faisalabad Campus (CFD) – Chiniot Campus
How often is the schedule updated?
Timetable records on Fastable are synced continuously with academic office updates. During the start of the semester and the course add/drop registration week, schedules are refreshed multiple times daily to capture room swaps, section mergers, instructor substitutions, and lab adjustments.
Can I check room allocations and teacher slots?
Yes! Each class slot explicitly includes the room allocation (e.g., Room C-301, Civil Block Lab 2, Main Auditorium) and the assigned instructor. You can use the search bar to enter any room code (such as "Lab 4") or instructor's name to view their scheduled classes and identify vacant room periods.
How do I download the FASTable Android APK for offline use?
You can download the verified Android APK package directly from the GitHub release portal using the Download button on this page. Once downloaded:
  1. Open the downloaded FASTable-v1.0.7.apk file on your Android phone.
  2. Enable "Install from this source" in your phone's security settings if prompted.
  3. Complete installation and launch FASTable to save your section timetable offline.
